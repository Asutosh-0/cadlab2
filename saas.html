SaaS · WebGIS App

From drone data to a live spatial view.

The blueprint describes an end-to-end solution designed for drone professionals for inspection, real-time tracking and monitoring, creating 3D models, orthomosaic image generation, image analysis and volume calculations in a single platform.

  • Drone / UAV and LiDAR data processing
  • 3D model and orthomosaic generation
  • Inspection, tracking and monitoring
  • Image analysis and volume calculations
  • Spatial analysis, visualisation and data management
