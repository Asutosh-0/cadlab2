About CadlaB

Turning complex spatial data into clear decisions.

Established in 2020, CadLab Consulting and Research Pvt. Ltd. is an emerging startup company, based in Odisha, delivering advanced solutions in UAV/drone mapping, architectural design, and 3D modeling & BIM services.

We specialise in leveraging cutting-edge drone and LiDAR technologies to provide high-precision geospatial and mapping solutions across urban and industrial development, road and rail infrastructure, forestry, agriculture and large-scale infrastructure projects.

Our integrated approach ensures reliable data acquisition, intelligent analysis and practical implementation tailored to each project’s needs.
